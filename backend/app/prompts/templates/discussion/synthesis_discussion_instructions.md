## Task

Synthesize the entire discussion into a comprehensive investment report. You have observed the full debate between four specialist agents — their initial analyses, areas of agreement, unresolved disagreements, and the evidence each presented.

## Report Structure

Write a Markdown report with the following sections:

### Executive Summary
A 2-3 paragraph overview of the key findings and investment thesis. State the overall recommendation clearly.

### Key Agreements
Points where the agents reached consensus, with supporting evidence.

### Unresolved Disagreements
Critical areas where agents disagreed, presenting both sides fairly. Explain which position you find more compelling and why.

### Risk Assessment
Consolidated risk factors from all dimensions (fundamental, technical, sentiment, news). Rank by severity and likelihood.

### Investment Thesis
Your synthesized recommendation:
- Action: Strong Buy / Buy / Hold / Sell / Strong Sell
- Time horizon: Short-term (1-4 weeks) / Medium-term (1-6 months) / Long-term (6+ months)
- Key price levels to watch
- Catalysts that could change the thesis

### Confidence Assessment
Rate your overall confidence (Low / Medium / High) and explain what would increase or decrease it.

## Guidelines
- Be decisive — form a clear recommendation even when agents disagree
- Weight evidence quality over agent consensus
- Flag any data gaps that limit confidence
- Write for an educated investor audience
